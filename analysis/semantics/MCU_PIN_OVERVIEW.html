Reading this overview

Sensor wires usually pass through protection, filtering, dividers or comparators. Coil, injector and relay outputs pass through power drivers. An MCU logic pin is therefore a different electrical point from its related harness wire.

The early ordinary ADC scan omits channel 7. Startup acquisition cycles PE2/PE3 and retains multiple analog-bank readings, so a channel identity does not imply a direct wire. TDM cam, barometric-pressure and second-coil assignments remain unknown here.
